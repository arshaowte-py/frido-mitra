Welcome

Welcome to Frido Mitra.

You've joined a network of people who help Indians move better and hurt less — traders, physiotherapists, doctors, creators and students who put Frido in front of the people who need it most.

Being a Mitra isn't only about selling. It's about your voice, your audience and your credibility. We give you the products, the payouts and the playbook — you make it your own.

Glad to have you with us.

01 — Onboarding

Start here

Four things to do in your first week. None of them take long.

  1. Log in to your Refer Rush dashboard. The welcome email we sent on approval has your access link. Inside you'll find your coupon code and your referral link, both generated for you automatically.
  2. Add your payout details. PAN and bank account — see Getting paid. Do this before your first sale so nothing is held up later.
  3. Meet your Affiliate Manager. Every Mitra is assigned an AM and a category when approved. Your AM is your single point of contact for hooks, product picks and anything that goes wrong.
  4. Join the WhatsApp community. The invite is in your welcome email. See Community.
Your category

When your application was approved, you were tagged into a category — GT trader, physiotherapist or doctor, established creator, or new creator. Your category decides which products get recommended to you, which hooks your AM sends, and which schemes you're eligible for.

If you think you're in the wrong category, tell your AM. It can be changed.

03 — Your dashboard

Your portal

Everything about your performance lives in one place.

  • Your code and link — copy either with one tap.
  • Your orders — every order placed on your code, with its current state. If an order was cancelled or returned, you'll see that here, which is usually the answer to "why wasn't I paid for this sale?"

04 — Payouts

Getting paid

Straight to your UPI. Fully compliant, with your tax paperwork handled.

Setting up

We deliberately do not ask for PAN and bank details at signup — that's friction before you've earned anything. You add them when you make your first withdrawal.

  1. Request your payout from the wallet section of your dashboard once you have a claimable balance.
  2. Enter your PAN. It's verified in real time and we pull your registered name and address from it.
  3. Add your bank account. Your name on the PAN is matched against the name on the bank account.
  4. Receive the money on your UPI ID.
The name on the account must be yours

Your PAN, your bank account and your UPI must all belong to you. If you submit a parent's, spouse's or friend's PAN or account, the name-match check will fail and the payout will not go through.

This isn't bureaucracy for its own sake — paying one person against another person's PAN is non-compliant for Frido and creates a tax problem for you. The check exists to protect both of us from fraud.

Holding period

Commission doesn't become claimable the instant an order is placed. It sits in maturing first, so the order can be delivered and the return window can close. You'll see the maturing balance and the claimable balance separately in your dashboard.

TBC The exact holding period for Frido Mitra is not yet fixed. For reference, comparable programs commonly run 30–60 day net terms; Frido's number should be set against actual return and RTO windows rather than copied.

Tax — handled for you

TDS is deducted and tracked automatically, and you get a Form 16A for it. You'll receive periodic email reminders showing exactly what has been deducted, so you can download it and file without chasing anyone.

You do not need to reconcile anything by hand. This is one of the main reasons Frido chose this payout stack.

06 — Guidelines

Brand guidelines

Frido sells comfort and pain relief. That places real limits on what we can claim.

Do

  • Talk about your own experience, honestly.
  • Show the product in real use — walking, sitting, working, sleeping.
  • Be specific about which problem it solves.
  • Say clearly that it's a paid or affiliate partnership.
  • Use the assets and hooks your AM shares.

Don't

  • Promise that Frido cures, treats or prevents any medical condition.
  • Invent results, timelines or statistics.
  • Disparage other brands by name.
  • Alter Frido's logo, packaging or product photography.
  • Quote prices or discounts that aren't currently live.
If you are a healthcare professional

Physiotherapists and doctors carry a higher bar than creators. Recommend Frido as a comfort or support product — never as a treatment, and never as a substitute for clinical care. Follow your own professional body's rules on endorsement; they override anything on this page.

07 — Content

Filming

Read this before you shoot, not after.

Before you film

  • Check the product is still live. Ask your AM for the current discontinued list — nothing wastes a good video like promoting something that's out of stock.
  • Pick your category first, then the product, then the hook. Your AM sends the hooks that are working this week.
  • Shoot vertical, in good light, with clean audio.
  • Show the product doing its job. Static shots don't convert.

Getting the click

Your link belongs in your bio and your code belongs in the video and the caption. Say the code out loud — people remember what they hear.

TBC Whether Instagram link-in-comment or an in-reel CTA performs better for Frido is an open A/B test V0, as is whether to cross-post to YouTube Shorts for product tagging. Your AM will share results as they land.

The flow

Pick your category → check the product is live → get the current hook from your AM → film using the rules above → post with your code and link → tell your AM it's up.

09 — Ground rules

Ground rules

Short list. Breaking these costs you your code and your unpaid commission.

  • Don't post your code on coupon or deal-sharing sites. Not on deal forums, not on coupon aggregators, not on Reddit. Codes leak from those places and get used by people who were never your audience — which drains your commission and Frido's margin at once.
  • Don't drop your code on another creator's content. You're taking a sale someone else earned. This is the fastest way to lose your code.
  • One Mitra, one code. Applying for a second code under another name or number is grounds for immediate removal.
  • Don't run paid ads on Frido's brand terms without written permission from your AM. You'd be bidding against Frido's own spend.
  • Share your code with your audience — social platforms, your clinic, your shop, your campus. That's the whole point.
Consequence

Breaching these guidelines results in your code being deactivated and any unpaid commission forfeited. Where the breach is honest and one-off, your AM will talk to you first — but leaked codes get shut down immediately, because every hour they stay live costs money.

10 — Questions

FAQ

Does my customer have to use my link, or is my code enough?

Both your code and your link are tracked, and orders placed on your code appear in your dashboard.

TBC Exactly how attribution resolves when someone types your code without clicking your link — and what the cookie window is — needs confirming with Refer Rush before this page is published. Don't guess at it with your audience; ask your AM.

I made a sale but I haven't been paid for it.

Open your orders list. The usual reasons are that the order was cancelled or returned, or that the commission is still maturing. Both states are shown against the order.

If the order shows as delivered and matured and you still haven't been paid, message your AM.

My customer got a "usage limit reached" error on my code.

Usually this means the code has been shared beyond your own audience and hit its cap — see Ground rules. Tell your AM straight away rather than waiting; if it has leaked, the sooner it's rotated the less you lose.

Do I need a PAN card?

Yes, to withdraw. You can join, promote and earn without one, but the money can't leave the platform until PAN and a matching bank account are verified.

Can I use a family member's bank account?

No. The name on your PAN and the name on your bank account must match. Payouts against someone else's account fail the check and won't be released.

Can I be a Mitra for another brand at the same time?

Yes, unless you've signed something specific with Frido that says otherwise. What you can't do is promote a directly competing product inside the same piece of content as Frido.

Who do I contact when something goes wrong?

Your Affiliate Manager, first and always. They were assigned to you at approval and their contact is in your welcome email. Payouts, orders, samples, category changes and code problems all go through them.
