## Brand
Frido is an Indian ergonomic and orthopedic comfort brand selling products for back, knee and foot pain,
such as orthotic insoles, seat cushions and posture supports, mainly online.

## The offer being tested (assumed)
"Frido Care" is a bundle of an orthotic insole plus a seat cushion, with a free 15-minute online physiotherapist consultation,
priced at ₹2,499 (assumed). It is sold through the Frido website and affiliate links.

## Launch message (assumed)
"Sitting all day? Standing all day? Frido Care fixes the posture behind your pain — product plus a real physio, ₹2,499."
