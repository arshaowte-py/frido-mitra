# Frido Care — Seed v1 for MiroFish

> Internal planning material for simulation. All people below are fictional composites, not real individuals.
> Items marked (OPEN) are not decided yet; the simulation should treat them as uncertain.

## 1. Frido
Frido is an Indian ergonomic and orthopedic comfort brand (Pune). It sells insoles, orthopedic footwear and sandals,
knee caps, lumbar belts, heel protectors, cushions, pillows, anti-slip washroom products, ergonomic chairs and furniture,
mostly online (its own website and Amazon) and through some retail stores. Typical everyday products cost ₹900–₹2,000.

## 2. The problem Frido Care solves
- Most Indian desk workers report work-related musculoskeletal pain; many elderly people live with pain needing treatment.
- India has far fewer physiotherapists per person than the WHO benchmark.
- Physios and doctors recommend products verbally; the patient forgets, buys the wrong size on Amazon, and the physio gets no credit.
- Contact between patient and physio usually ends at the last session.

## 3. What Frido Care is
Frido Care is an app connecting practitioners, patients and Frido.
- **Practitioner app:** a physio or doctor onboards (with registration number), assesses the patient (pain body map, complaints,
  history), then writes ONE digital prescription containing Frido products, Frido services and home exercises (sets and reps).
  The prescription is sent to the patient on WhatsApp. The physio has a patient logbook, a referral passbook showing every
  rupee earned, a personal discount code, and Frido Academy courses to become a Frido Certified Physiotherapist.
- **Patient app:** the patient keeps their own copy of the prescription, buys suggested products with 10% off (delivered home),
  books services, follows exercises, and the physio stays in touch. Progress can be shared with the treating doctor.

- **Consent and data:** patients consent before their health details are stored; the patient-physio relationship and data
  ownership between Frido, the physio and the network is a sensitive point.
- **Support for practitioners:** sample kit and QR cards in every bag, a Frido Care rep visits every ~21 days, zone WhatsApp
  groups and a leaderboard. Practitioners with zero orders after 45 days are dropped from the active list.

## 4. Services (prices not final, expected ₹1,000–₹2,000 each)
Ergonomic workspace assessment (on-site or virtual desk, chair and posture audit), personalised footwear,
2D scan gait analysis, 3D Volumental foot scan with made-to-measure insole, sleep and bed assessment,
general physio consultation, full body assessment. Later: post-op rehabilitation programmes.

## 5. Money rules
- **Patient:** 10% off products ordered through the practitioner's code.
- **Consultation fee:** the patient pays a consultation fee (around ₹800–₹1,000). If, after the consultation, the patient buys
  products above a certain amount, the fee is rebated to the patient and Frido bears the consultation cost. (Threshold OPEN.)
- **Practitioner commission on products:** around 10–12% of each attributed order; a solo physio can reach 15% at a higher tier.
  Paid monthly into an in-app wallet (tax deducted at source), only after delivery and the return window. The physio gets a
  same-day message when a patient orders. Possible ₹500 first-order bonus and leaderboard prizes. Late or missed payouts
  would quickly destroy physio trust.
- **Centres and networks (super affiliates):** 3–5% override on every order by their member physios.
- **Doctors registered with the medical council:** cannot receive cash for recommending products; they get non-cash benefits
  only (samples, listing, CME courses), or rewards go to the clinic entity.
- **Leads brought by Frido:** Frido also routes its own existing customers and customers acquired through Meta (Facebook/Instagram)
  ads to partner physios for a consultation or service. The physio who serves them still earns product commission, but may
  receive a lower consultation fee because Frido brought the lead. (OPEN — being considered.)
- **Frido's own staff:** Frido employs certified consultants plus physios and podiatrists who also consult on Frido Care.
  Partner physios are independent, not Frido employees.

## 6. Channels
- **Home physios (volume):** see 6–7 patients a day, the same patient 8–10 times. They carry samples (knee cap, lumbar belt,
  insole) in their bag. An assessment (consultation fee rebated if the patient buys above the threshold) can lead to a large recommended basket (up to around ₹30,000 for a full
  ergonomic setup) plus everyday products around ₹1,100.
- **Ortho and sports-medicine doctors (high ticket):** back bundle (~₹4,800) and desk-posture bundle (~₹6,600) via a code
  printed on the prescription pad; referrals to assessments.
- **Physio networks and home-physio firms (multiplier):** e.g. Healyos, a home-physiotherapy company in Pune and Navi Mumbai
  with about 175 physios, is the first network partner; more networks, chains and hospital departments will follow.
- **Silver Community (later):** seniors and their adult children; a Home Safe Kit (fall prevention for bath, bed, seating),
  a quarterly care plan with re-assessments and a family view; senior-living operators as B2B partners. First pilot in Bengaluru.

**Partners**
- Sanjay, 42, head of a home-physio network with 150+ physios, evaluating the 3–5% override; worried Frido might poach
  his physios or his patients and about who owns patient data.

**Competing options in their minds**
Buying cheaper generic supports from a chemist or Amazon; painkillers; other orthopedic brands; physio clinics that sell
their own products; doing nothing.

## 8. Open questions this simulation should explore
1. Do patients see a physio's Frido prescription as care or as a sales pitch? What builds or breaks trust?
2. Does the "consultation fee rebated if you buy above a threshold" rule feel fair, or like pressure to buy?
3. Will physios actively recommend, and what stops them (reputation, app effort, payout trust, commission size)?
4. How do network heads react to the override, and to Frido routing its own leads to their physios at a lower fee?
5. Do Meta-ad leads actually show up for a paid or free assessment and buy afterwards? Where do they drop off?
